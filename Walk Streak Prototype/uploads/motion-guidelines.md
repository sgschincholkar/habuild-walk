# motion-guidelines.md

## Motion Philosophy
- Motion should feel calm, soft, and reassuring.
- Avoid gamified or hyper-energetic transitions.
- Prioritize emotional smoothness over flashy interactions.

## Transition Timing
- Standard transition: 200ms ease-out
- Slightly slower for onboarding: 250ms
- Never use aggressive spring animations

## Screen Transitions
- Use soft fade + slide transitions
- New screens slide gently upward
- Avoid hard cuts

## Bottom Sheets
- Slide up from bottom
- Slight background dim
- Rounded top corners
- Close with downward swipe or tap outside

## Button Interactions
- Slight scale-down on tap
- Soft elevation increase on press
- No bounce effects

## Cards
- Subtle lift on interaction
- Minimal hover behavior
- Mobile-first touch interactions prioritized

## Loading States
- Use shimmer skeletons
- Soft pulse animation
- Avoid spinner-only loading states

## Celebration Motion
- Gentle success transitions
- Minimal confetti
- Subtle streak highlights only

## Navigation
- Bottom nav transitions softly
- Preserve scroll positions
- Avoid abrupt state resets

## AI Coach
- Coach cards fade in smoothly
- Avoid chatbot typing animations

## Motion Avoidance
- No parallax
- No flashy microinteractions
- No exaggerated easing
- No rapid motion
- No fitness-app energy
