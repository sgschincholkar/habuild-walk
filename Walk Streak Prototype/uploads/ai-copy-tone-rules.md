# ai-copy-tone-rules.md

## Product Voice
- Calm
- Warm
- Encouraging
- Simple
- Human
- Non-judgmental

## Writing Principles
- Use short sentences
- Use practical encouragement
- Focus on consistency, not achievement
- Keep copy emotionally safe

## Good Examples
- “A short walk still counts.”
- “You showed up today.”
- “Keep your rhythm simple.”
- “Tomorrow is another chance to walk.”
- “A gentle walk is enough today.”

## Avoid
- “Crush your goals.”
- “No excuses.”
- “Push harder.”
- “Burn calories.”
- “Stay disciplined.”
- “You failed your streak.”

## AI Coach Tone
- Practical
- Weather-aware
- Calm
- Helpful
- Specific

## Weather Guidance
- Helpful, not dramatic
- Focus on comfort and safety

## Missed Walk Tone
- Recovery-focused
- No guilt
- No shame

## Celebration Tone
- Gentle and grounded
- Celebrate showing up
- Avoid loud excitement

## Notification Tone
- Short
- Clear
- Human
- Non-spammy

## Error Tone
- Calm
- Reassuring
- Never technical

## CTA Tone
- Soft encouragement
- Action-oriented without pressure

## Reading Level
- Simple everyday language
- Easy for broad Indian audience
