# illustration-guidelines.md

## Illustration Philosophy
- Illustrations should feel emotionally warm and human.
- Focus on consistency and reassurance.
- Avoid fitness culture aesthetics.

## Style
- Soft flat illustration style
- Rounded shapes
- Gentle color palette
- Minimal line detail
- Friendly facial expressions

## Themes
- Walking
- Morning sunlight
- Calm movement
- Nature
- Breathability
- Habit-building
- Consistency

## Characters
- Inclusive Indian audience representation
- Women 35–60+ prominently represented
- Everyday clothing
- Relaxed body language
- Avoid athletic physiques

## Emotional Tone
- Encouraging
- Peaceful
- Safe
- Warm
- Supportive

## Avoid
- Gym imagery
- Workout intensity
- Sportswear aesthetics
- Competitive visuals
- Extreme fitness poses
- Hyper-polished 3D renders

## Color Usage
- Use muted greens and warm neutrals
- Accent yellow only sparingly
- Avoid high saturation

## Background Elements
- Organic blobs
- Leaves
- Soft gradients
- Rounded landscape shapes

## Empty States
- Minimal illustrations
- Avoid over-detailing
- Keep emotional clarity strong
