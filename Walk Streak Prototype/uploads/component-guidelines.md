# component-guidelines.md

## General Principles
- Components should feel calm, soft, and approachable.
- Reuse components consistently across all screens.
- Prefer fewer component types with strong consistency.

## Buttons
### Primary Button
- Background: #2D6A4F
- Text: white
- Fully rounded pill
- Height: 48–56px
- Strong visual hierarchy

### Secondary Button
- White or transparent surface
- Primary border/text
- Softer emphasis

### Destructive Button
- Use #E53E3E sparingly
- Never dominant unless necessary

## Cards
- Background: white
- Rounded corners large
- Warm subtle shadow
- Spacious internal padding
- Stack vertically

## Bottom Navigation
- Fixed position
- Rounded top container
- Soft elevation
- Active tab highlighted using primary green
- Minimal icons with labels

## Chips & Toggles
- Fully rounded
- Large touch targets
- Selected state uses soft green tint
- Avoid harsh outlined chips

## AI Coach Card
- White card surface
- Soft contextual badge
- Practical short copy
- One primary CTA maximum

## Progress Calendar
- Inspired by Streaks app
- Rounded day pills
- Gentle completion highlights
- Avoid dense graph visuals

## Empty States
- Simple illustration
- One clear CTA
- Encouraging tone

## Modals & Bottom Sheets
- Rounded top corners
- Slide-up interaction
- Spacious padding
- Keep copy minimal

## Inputs
- Large readable inputs
- Soft borders
- Avoid compact forms
- Mobile-friendly keyboard spacing

## Shadows
- Warm and soft only
- No hard dark shadows
- Maintain subtle depth hierarchy
